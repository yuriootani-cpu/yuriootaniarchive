Codex

I lived in a house that sat on a hill, on a little lane across from a church.
I remember evenings gathered around the TV with my family.
The reading corner near the front door always sat empty, so I claimed it as mine.
The quietest spot in the house.
I found myself obsessed with a memoir, written by a woman with many lovers.
The book was titled “Codex.”
It was written under the pseudonym Astra.
It was no surprise she’d captivated so many lovers.
Every episode was incredibly intimate.
As if she had a photographic memory
for every word spoken between them.

I read every affair
from inside the lover’s skin.
I wanted what they wanted.
I thought what they thought.
Her prose was so beautiful,
I let it possess me.
I wanted her to be talking to me.
So I started talking back.

About halfway through…
