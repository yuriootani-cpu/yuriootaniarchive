Kibbe/Kitchener facial analysis study

559FACES
3 SHAPES

i was curious whether kibbe and kitchener facial types ("you're a soft natural," "that's a high-spirited face") are grounded in anything real, or purely vibe based. so i turned 559 faces into 3d head shapes and checked.

short answer: it doesn't. but the faces did sort themselves into three shapes, and i still can't explain one of them.

From my notebook×

started with a way bigger pile of random faces (13k+) but it got too big, so i cut it down to three groups: western celebrities the style community already typed, k-pop idols and korean actresses, and world leaders. no photos on this page. everything here is built from the shape numbers.

01The typing systems

kibbe & kitchener vs. actual face geometry

every celebrity here already had a kibbe type (soft natural, dramatic, etc.) or kitchener essence (romantic, high-spirited, etc.) assigned by the style community. i ran each photo through DECA, a model that rebuilds a 3d head from a single photo and strips out expression, pose and lighting, so you're left with just the shape of the face.

if the types were really in the bones, same-type faces should sit near each other. simplest check: find each face's closest lookalike by shape and see if they share a type.

every kibbe type shows up in every shape group

rows are kibbe types, columns are where those faces landed. if kibbe tracked geometry, each row would pile into one column. none of them do.

for reference, here's what the theory expects: the yin/yang map kibbe is built on.

03What it found instead

west-leaning, east-leaning, and a third thing

so i took the labels off and let k-means sort the faces by shape alone. three groups came out. two of them roughly follow where people are from. the third doesn't follow anything i measured.

who ended up where

these are leanings, not ethnicities. 59 western celebs landed east-leaning and 32 k-pop idols and actresses landed west-leaning. obama, modi and moon jae-in went west-leaning, while xi jinping and shinzo abe went east-leaning.

the third shape

this is the weird one. if you only let the algorithm make two groups, it doesn't split east vs. west. it splits this third shape off from everyone else, and that's the cleanest split in the whole dataset (). no idea what it is yet tbh. it pulls faces from all three groups:

04The faces

each group's average head, turned up 5×

each head is the group's average 3d shape, with its difference from the overall average multiplied by five so you can actually see it. flip to 1× to see how subtle it really is.

05Fine print

what this can't tell you

  • the groups aren't balanced. the celebrities are all women and the leaders are almost all men (46 of 49), mostly older. a lot of the western celebs come from old studio portraits. some of what separates the groups could be sex, age or photo era, not ancestry.
  • "western" is loose. it's whoever the kibbe community has typed: mostly american and european, but also madhubala, awkwafina, frida kahlo and others.
  • kitchener is thin. only 41 faces had kitchener labels. on one measure the labels are slightly less scattered than random (p = ), but they still don't form groups.
  • it's geometry only. stylists might be picking up on something real that 3d shape doesn't capture, like coloring, skin texture, body frame or just the overall vibe.
  • the groups are soft. silhouette scores of 0.18–0.30 mean real tendencies with a lot of overlap, not boxes. faces vary continuously, which lines up with the face-space idea from academic research (valentine, 1991).
  • how, exactly: one frontal photo per person → DECA → 100 FLAME shape numbers → PCA to 10 → k-means (k=3) and a 3d UMAP. type tests are nearest-neighbor match, silhouette and ARI, with p-values from 500 label shuffles.

data

faces.csv has every face with its group, shape, kibbe/kitchener label and map position. flame-shape-params.csv has the 100 raw shape numbers per face.
